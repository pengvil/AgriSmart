A Farmer can:
	
	1. Register an account to use the AgriSmart application with a personal profile
	2. Log in securely to access farming information
	3. Create and update a farming profile
	4. Search for crop information including care, season, soil type, and basic farming methods
	5. Set farming reminders for tasks like watering, fertilizing, or harvesting
	6. View weather notices to plan farming activities
	7. View crop market prices to decide when and where to sell crops
	8. Select a crop problem to view suggestions and get basic help
        9. Upload photos of crop diseases for diagnosis.
       10. Maintain a farm activity diary and record farming operations.
       11. Track expenses, investments, and crop profits.
       12. Receive notifications about government agricultural programs and subsidies.
       13. Access modern farming techniques and training materials.
       14. Connect with agricultural experts for advice.
